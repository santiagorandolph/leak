    How it works

    1. 01

      Export a CSV

      Most banks have a “Download transactions” or “Export CSV” button in their activity page.

    2. 02

      Drop it here

      Columns are detected automatically. Amounts, dates, and merchant names are normalized in memory.

    3. 03

      See the yearly cost

      Charges that repeat monthly or less often are grouped and annualized, so $15.49/month reads as $185/year.

    What it does — and doesn't — catch

    Leak looks for the same merchant charging a similar amount on a monthly, bimonthly, quarterly, semiannual, or yearly rhythm. Weekly habits like coffee runs are deliberately ignored. One-time annual charges that appear only once in your export can't be detected, which is why a longer statement works better.
